Selected work代表作

Two agent skills. Hand either one a photo and ask.两个 agent skill，用法都一样：丢一张照片进去，然后提问。

Read the photo. Catenary masts on the viaduct mean an electrified railway, the mountain is about 3 km away, and the rice is harvested while the grass is still green. South China, as a bet, not a proof.读图。高架上的杆子是接触网支柱，说明是电气化铁路；陡峭的山在约 3 公里外；稻子已收割，草还绿着。先押华南，是判断，不是证明。

Region scan. Every railway bridge in the region from OpenStreetMap, 27,335 segments, with a 360° horizon computed every 400 m. 171 sites have flat ground nearby, a clear mountain within a few km and a flat horizon beside it.区域扫描。从 OpenStreetMap 取出区域内所有铁路桥，共 27,335 段，每 400 米用高程数据算一次 360° 地平线。附近平坦、几公里内有明显山峰、旁边地平线平坦的，只剩 171 处。

Skyline fit. 14,372 camera positions, each rendered with the ridge line it would see, leave 22. The top three are drawn back onto the photo: #1 only scored well because the oven hides a bump; #2, Qingyuan, fits from the foot of the mountain to the edge of the frame.天际线拟合。14,372 个候选机位各自渲染出能看到的山脊线，剩下 22 个。前三名画回照片核对：第一名的凸起正好藏在烤炉后面；第二名清远从山脚到画面边缘都贴合。

Pier count. The 17 piers in the photo become 17 bearings from the camera, and where they meet the railway the intersections must be evenly spaced. One spot is left: ±2 m, with the direction the camera faced.数桥墩。照片里 17 根桥墩变成从机位出发的 17 条方位线，它们和铁路线的交点必须间距均匀。最后只剩一个点：误差 ±2 米，朝向也一并给出。

geo-sleuth

Finds where a photo was taken, and shows its work. When there is no sign, plate or landmark to read, OpenStreetMap geometry, elevation data, satellite tiles and street view carry the search. Every conclusion points at the command that produced it.

找出一张照片是在哪儿拍的，并把推理过程摊开给你看。照片里没有招牌、车牌、地标可读时，就靠 OpenStreetMap 几何、高程数据、卫星图和街景来搜。每个结论都指向产出它的那条命令。

27,335 → 171 → 22 → 3 → 1

Candidates left after each step, from every railway bridge in the region to one camera position.

每一步之后剩下的候选：从区域内所有铁路桥，一直收窄到一个相机位置。

Holmes 福尔摩斯

Reads a photo the way Sherlock Holmes reads a stranger. It lists what it can see and seals the list before it reasons. Each conclusion cites the observations it rests on and offers another explanation, and then you grade it.

像福尔摩斯读陌生人一样读一张照片。先把看到的东西列出来、封存清单，然后才开始推理。每条结论都引用它依据的观察，并给出另一种可能的解释，最后由你来打分。

03

Research研究

Knowledge graph question answering: let a small model do most of the work, and call the large one only when it is needed.知识图谱问答：大部分活交给小模型，只在确实需要时才调用大模型。

AACL-IJCNLP 2026 · Main ConferenceAACL-IJCNLP 2026 · 主会

RouterKGQA: Specialized–General Model Routing for Constraint-Aware Knowledge Graph Question Answering

Bo Yuan*, Hexuan Deng*, Xuebo Liu, Min Zhang · *equal contribution· *共同一作

arXiv:2603.20017 ↗ PDF ↗

A small specialized model drafts the reasoning path over the knowledge graph. When that path cannot be reached in the graph, a large general model is called in to repair it. Accuracy goes up while the cost stays close to the small model's.

小的专用模型先在知识图谱上生成推理路径；路径在图里走不通时，才调用大的通用模型来修。准确率更高，开销仍接近只用小模型。

backprop-viz

A 3D walkthrough of a GPT's backward pass. Every block sits where it sits in the forward pass but holds the gradient instead of the tensor. The numbers are computed live and checked against numerical derivatives.

GPT 反向传播的 3D 交互演示。每个方块的位置和前向传播时一样，装的却是梯度而不是张量。数字是实时算出来的，并用数值导数校验过。

Built on Brendan Bycroft's llm-viz.基于 Brendan Bycroft 的 llm-viz。

Anima

Seven anime characters share a seaside town, and each one is its own LLM agent. Every tick they decide what to do next from what they need, who they remember and what they can see. Friendships, grudges and gossip come out of the interactions.
